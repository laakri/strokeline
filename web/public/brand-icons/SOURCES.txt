Brand SVGs in this directory are generated from the Simple Icons CDN using the
catalog in web/scripts/brand-icons.json. Regenerate them with `npm run icons:brands`
from the web directory. Source: https://cdn.simpleicons.org/ and
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

Each logo and brand name remains the property of its respective owner. Simple
Icons asks users to review its brand-specific disclaimer and verify permitted
uses: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md
